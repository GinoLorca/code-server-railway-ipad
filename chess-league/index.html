How the League Works

  • Conference An ICN program — Midtown, Upper East Side, Lower East Side, Brooklyn.
  • Team A partner school within that program.
  • Match A weekly round of boards between two schools.
  • Points 2 for a match win, 1 for a draw, 0 for a loss.
  • ICN Cup Top 4 per conference qualify for end‑of‑season playoffs.
